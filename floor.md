## Floor Leveling Execution Plan

### 1. Seal the Perimeter Drainage System & Walls
* **Attach Wall Expansion Foam:** Staple or tape the 5-1/2" foam sill sealer around the lower perimeter of all drywall surfaces. This gives the cement room to expand and prevents it from bonding to the walls.
* **Vacuum the concrete:** Vacuum thoroughly along the baseboard drain channel.
* **Build the Spray Foam Dam:** Draw a reference line exactly 1 inch in front of your plastic drain channel. Spray a continuous, 2-inch high bead of Great Stuff expanding foam directly onto the concrete along that line.
* **Let the Dam Cure:** Allow the spray foam to sit for 1 to 2 hours until it expands and hardens into a rigid, sticky wall. This will flawlessly hold back the liquid cement.

### 2. Encapsulate the Cutback Adhesive
* **Roll the primer:** Pour the MAPEI EcoPrim Grip into your paint tray. Using 3/8" microfiber rollers, roll an even coat over the entire floor directly over the black adhesive residue.
* **Avoid puddles:** Ensure the primer is rolled out fully without leaving thick, milky puddles in low spots.
* **Let it tack:** Wait 1 to 3 hours. The floor is ready when the primer feels rough, dry, and tacky to the touch (like liquid sandpaper).

### 3. Establish the Mixing Station
* **Plumb the water:** Turn off the washing machine cold water valve, disconnect the washer hose, and thread on the garden hose with the shut-off valve attached. Turn the supply valve on and test for drips.
* **Stage the equipment:** Plug in the rented 1/2" heavy-duty corded mixing drill. Stack all 10 bags of TEC EZ Level 323 adjacent to the mixing station and slice open the first 3 bags.
* **Measure water:** Use the 10-quart mixing bucket to measure exactly **5.0 to 5.25 quarts (160–168 oz)** of cold water into your first 5-gallon bucket.

### 4. The Continuous Pour Rotation (3-Person Team)

**Role Division:**
* **Person 1 (Water & Powder Loader):** Measures exact water into the bucket and slowly pours the 50 lb bag into the water while Person 2 mixes.
* **Person 2 (Drill Operator):** Operates the heavy-duty corded mixing drill on low speed with both hands, mixing for 2 to 3 minutes until completely lump-free.
* **Person 3 (Pourer & Finisher):** Manages floor placement and squeegee work.

**Center-Door Pour Strategy:**
1. **Start Far Left Wing:** Person 3 takes the first 2–3 buckets to the far left corner away from the door, placing ribbons of material and gauging with the 3/16" notched squeegee.
2. **Move to Far Right Wing:** Pour the next 2–3 buckets in the far right corner, spreading back toward the center.
3. **Bridge the Center Back Wall:** Pour along the back wall center, merging the left and right sides into a continuous wet edge.
4. **Funnel Back to Door Threshold:** Work backward down the middle of the room toward the open doorway, making the final pour at the threshold.

### 5. Immediate Cleanup and Curing
* **Wash tools immediately:** The instant the 10th bucket is poured, flush the mixing paddle, squeegee, and buckets with the hose before the cement sets.
* **Lock the room down:** Keep doors closed. Do not run heavy fans or space heaters directly over the uncured pour to prevent surface cracking.
* **Wait 24 hours:** Allow the floor to cure undisturbed overnight.

### 6. Post-Cure Prep for LVP
* **Remove the Dam:** After 24 hours, take your utility knife, lay the blade flush against your new cement floor, and slice straight down to cut the spray foam away. This leaves a clean 1-inch gap for your drain.
* **Trim Wall Foam:** Use the utility knife to slice off the excess perimeter sill sealer flush with the top of the new floor.
* **Check levelness:** Lay your 5-foot spirit level across the floor to verify flatness.
* **Sweep and install:** Vacuum any loose debris before laying down the vapor barrier and LVP.

## LVP Installation Execution Plan

### 0. Cutting Techniques: Score-and-Snap vs. Jigsaw
* **Straight Cross-Cuts (Short edge):** Measure, lay the rafter square, and score the top wear-layer 2–3 times with a utility knife. Bend the plank back over your knee to snap it, then slice the backing underneath.
* **Straight Rip Cuts (Long edge):** Score heavily 4–5 times using a straightedge. Place the scored seam over the edge of a sturdy table or workbench and press down firmly to snap the length.
* **Notches & L-Cuts (Furnace Pad & Door Trim):** Use the brought jigsaw fitted with a fine-tooth blade. Cut notches directly without scoring to prevent accidental diagonal cracking across the plank face.

### 1. Prep and Moisture Barrier
* **Verify Material:** Confirm an extra 1–2 boxes of LVP are available to account for waste around the furnace pad.
* **Clean the slab:** Vacuum the level floor thoroughly.
* **Roll out the poly:** Unroll 6-mil plastic sheeting, overlapping seams by at least 8 inches, and seal every joint with red Tuck Tape.
* **Run up perimeter:** Extend the plastic 2 inches up perimeter walls and furnace pad edges. Trim excess after flooring is installed.

### 2. Layout Strategy and Chalk Reel
* **Acclimate:** Store unopened LVP boxes flat in the installation room for 24–48 hours prior to starting.
* **Determine starting width:** Measure room width and divide by 7 inches. If the remainder is under 3.5 inches, plan to rip-cut the first row to ensure balanced end rows.
* **Secure plastic & place tape:** Weigh down the plastic with LVP boxes. Apply a strip of painter's tape along the planned chalk line zone (~4.25 inches out from the wall).
* **Snap line:** Measure 4.25 inches out from both corners (plank width + 1/4" expansion gap). Stretch the chalk reel line taut across the painter's tape and snap a clean reference guide.

### 3. Laying the First Row
* **Cut wall taper:** Position planks along the chalk line, measuring back to the wall at both ends of each plank. Subtract 1/4" for expansion space, mark, and cut so the back edge matches wall contour while the front edge stays true to the chalk line.
* **Set spacers:** Place expansion spacers along all walls and rigid objects.
* **Lock ends:** Join short end seams by angling and locking planks together, tapping joints tight using a tapping block and dead-blow hammer.

### 4. Stagger & Main Floor Installation
* **Stagger joints:** Begin the second row using the cut off-piece from the first row's final plank (ensuring end joints are offset by at least 6–8 inches).
* **Angle & lock:** Insert long edges at a 20-degree angle, press forward, and drop flat into position.
* **Tap seams:** Tap long seams tight using a tapping block, then tap short seams level. Do not strike plank locking profiles directly.

### 5. Navigating the Furnace Slab
* **Maintain expansion space:** Treat the slab edge as a wall—place spacers around all sides.
* **Cut corners cleanly:** Use the jigsaw to execute clean L-notch cuts around furnace corners.
* **Align double runs:** When building around both sides of the furnace pad, ensure seams remain perfectly parallel so rows click together cleanly on the far side.

### 6. Finishing the Room
* **Measure final row:** Overlay a full plank on the second-to-last row. Use a scrap piece pushed against the wall to trace the wall contour onto the plank.
* **Rip & pull tight:** Cut along the marked line with a jigsaw or utility knife. Position the plank and use the pull bar with a dead-blow hammer to seat the final joint.
* **Install reducer:** Fasten the Framerica Surface Reducer mounting track to the concrete threshold, snapping the reducer cap into place over the expansion gap.
